Create

From lyrics to a finished song.

Describe the sound in your own words — genre, voice, instruments, mood, tempo — paste the lyrics with [Verse] and [Chorus] tags, and press create. A few minutes later you have a mixed song with vocals and a full band.

  • Lyrics in Vietnamese, English and many other languages
  • Male, female or duet vocals — or an instrumental of 1 to 5 minutes
  • Style ideas and templates to start from

Remix

Your recording, in a new style.

Upload a recording or pick a song you made. MelodiAI writes down its melody, chords and sections, fills in the lyrics for you to check, and plays it back as jazz, rock, a piano piece — whatever you describe.

  • Keep the original chords, or let it write new harmony
  • Sung or instrumental, with a new voice of your choice
  • MP3, WAV, FLAC or OGG, from 10 seconds to 6 minutes

Sheet music

Every song comes with its score.

See the melody, chords and lyrics on the staff, follow along on a piano or guitar view, swap the instruments and play it back, then take it to your own music software.

  • Print-ready PDF, MusicXML and MIDI
  • Render the score with real instrument sounds
  • Lossless FLAC download of every song

How it works

Three steps, no studio.

  1. 01

    Describe the sound

    A line about genre, voice, instruments and mood. Not sure? Start from one of the style ideas.

  2. 02

    Add the words

    Paste your lyrics with section tags, choose instrumental, or upload a recording to remix.

  3. 03

    Listen and take it with you

    Play it in your library, build playlists, and download the audio, the sheet music and the MIDI.

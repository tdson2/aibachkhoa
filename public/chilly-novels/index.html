Chilly Novels

Android · Vietnamese · offline reading

Download once. Read offline forever.

A small library of short stories and comics. Pick the ones you want, pull them onto the phone once, and they are yours to read on a plane, on the metro, or anywhere the signal gives up. A whole story weighs about fifteen kilobytes.

How it works

Three taps, then the network stops mattering.

The app opens on a shelf-building screen rather than a sign-up form. Nothing streams while you read, so a dead signal never costs you a chapter.

1Build the shelf

On first run the app shows the whole catalogue with a checkbox and a size beside each title, and adds up what you picked at the bottom. Take a couple, or take the lot — two stories came to 193 KB.

2Pull it down

One download and the text lives on the phone. Anything you skipped stays in the catalogue, and you can come back for it whenever you next have a connection.

3Read anywhere

Downloaded stories open with no network at all. Favourites, reading history and progress are written to the phone and never leave it.

The library

Two kinds of story, sorted the way you look for them.

The shelf holds prose and comics side by side, grouped into everyday life, humour, school and fantasy. The search sheet narrows it down without making you scroll.

  • Prose or comics. Filter to one or the other, or leave it on everything.
  • Sort that admits what it is. By title, by chapter count, or by how much space it will take.
  • Downloaded only. One switch hides everything you have not pulled down yet — the view you want on a train.
  • Genre chips. Everyday life, humour, school, fantasy — and per-story tags like adventure or detective.

Before you commit

Every story tells you its size first.

Open a title and you get the cover, the author, how many chapters there are and exactly how many kilobytes it will cost you — on the button itself, before you tap it.

  • A summary, not a teaser. Two lines telling you what the story actually is, so you can put it back.
  • Kept private on the device. Downloads sit in the app's own storage, encrypted, where nothing else on the phone reads them.
  • Favourites and progress. A heart on the story, a progress tab for what you have been through. Both stay on the phone.
  • No account, ever. There is no sign-up screen, because there is nothing to sign up to.

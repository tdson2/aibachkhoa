On iPhone and iPad

Drag to fly. The rest is nerve.

There is no fire button, no joystick and no menu between you and the next attempt. A thumb anywhere on the screen and the ship follows it, which leaves exactly one decision in the game: where to be.

One-handed, portrait

Steer by dragging anywhere. The cannon fires on its own, so nothing competes for the hand holding the phone.

Twenty gun tiers

Every power-up moves you up a rung, and each rung is a different weapon: scatter, piercing lances, plasma that splashes, homing swarms, and a nova cannon at the top.

A boss every fifth wave

Four waves of formations, then a health bar slides in. A boss throws a pattern you read rather than out-shoot.

Offline, and quiet

No account, no sign-in, no server, no advertising, no analytics. Scores stay on the device. The game never asks for a permission.

In-app purchases

Ships are paint. Nothing else is for sale.

Every purchase in Novaryn is cosmetic. No weapon, wave, difficulty or level is behind a payment, and the game is complete without spending anything. Purchases are handled by the App Store; we never see your payment details.

Ship skins — one-time purchases

Nine ships sold outright, from US$1.99 to US$5.99. Bought once, yours permanently, and restored on a new device with Restore Purchases in the shop.

Novaryn Pro — auto-renewable subscription

Length of subscription
1 month, renews automatically
Price
US$4.99 per month, billed in your local currency
What it unlocks
Every ship skin in the shop while the subscription is active

Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before that period ends. Manage or cancel it in Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Ships unlocked by an active subscription lock again when it ends; ships bought outright stay yours.

NOVARYN

Android · arcade space shooter

One thumb. Endless waves.

A vertical arcade shoot-'em-up made for short sessions. Drag anywhere on the screen to fly; your cannon fires by itself. Everything else is dodging, timing, and knowing when to push your luck.

The run

Drag to fly. The rest is nerve.

There is no fire button, no joystick and no menu between you and the next attempt. You put a thumb down anywhere on the glass and the ship follows it — which means the only decision left in the game is where to be, and that is the decision the whole thing is built around.

Anywhere on the screen is the stick

Not a pad in a corner your thumb has to find. Put a finger down where it already is and the ship tracks it one-to-one, so nothing you own is ever covered by your own hand at the moment you need to see it.

The cannon is never your problem

It fires on its own, at its own rate, for the whole run. Nothing is lost to a missed tap, and nothing is won by mashing — a run reads entirely as a line you flew, not a button you held.

Waves arrive, and keep arriving

Formations come in denser and faster each time, and there is nothing at the end to reach. The score counter and the BEST under it are the whole scoreboard: how far you got, against how far you have ever got.

One more try is eight seconds away

Death drops you straight back to the menu with START under your thumb. No unlock gate, no energy bar, no countdown — the loop is short enough to fit a bus stop and honest enough that a bad run is your fault.

Bosses

Sooner or later, something with a name.

Formations give way to a single ship that holds its ground, takes real punishment and attacks in shapes you have to read rather than out-shoot. Each fights like itself, and learning one is learning it for good.

No two of them open the same way

Each one has its own way of filling the screen, and its own tell before it does. Which is as much as is worth saying here — the rest is better met than read about.

Power-ups

Everything that helps you, you fly into.

Power-ups drop where the fighting was, so reaching one means going back into the space you were dodging a second ago. That trade is the game.

Nothing here is bought. Every power-up in Novaryn drops from play. There is nothing sold in the shop that helps you survive, no revive offered after a death and no advantage available for money — the shop holds ship skins and nothing else.

Ships

A shop of paint, and nothing else.

Skins change how your run looks and nothing about how it plays — no faster ship, no wider cannon, no extra life for money. A leaderboard where the top scores could be bought would not be worth reading.

Skins are one-time purchases. Google Play shows the shop in your own currency, and a skin you buy outright stays yours for good — on this device and on any other you sign in to with the same Google account.

Novaryn Pro

Optional, monthly, and spelled out.

Pro is a subscription that opens the whole shop and switches advertising off while it is active. It is the only recurring charge in the game, it changes nothing about how a run plays, and here is exactly what happens if you stop paying for it.

The question The answer
What it unlocksThe whole shop, for as long as the subscription is active — and advertising, which stops completely: with Pro the ad code is never started at all.
What it costs$4.99 a month on the US list, shown in your own currency at checkout and billed by Google Play.
What it does to a runNothing. No extra ship, no stronger cannon, no revive. Pro buys paint and quiet.
If you cancelShips unlocked through Pro lock again when it lapses. Ships you bought outright stay yours — cancelling never takes back a purchase.
How to cancelAny time, in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. We never see your card, and there is no cancellation flow inside the game to argue with you.

Built for a phone

Portrait, offline, and small.

Novaryn is shaped around the way a phone is actually held: upright, in one hand, for a few minutes at a time, often somewhere with no signal.

  • Portrait and one-handed. Playable while standing on a bus, with no rotation and no second thumb required.
  • Plays fully offline. The whole game runs on the device. Turn the connection off and nothing changes.
  • Small download, old hardware. Android 7.0 and up, and it does not ask a budget phone for more than it has.
  • Your best score is yours. It is kept on the device, in the game's own save file, and it is not sent anywhere.

Read the privacy policy

Which Android versions does it run on?

Android 7.0 and up, rated Everyone with in-game purchases declared.

Are there ads?

On Android, yes — between runs, never during one, and never a video you have to sit through to carry on playing. A Novaryn Pro subscription removes them completely. The iPhone and iPad version has no ads at all.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. A run plays identically with the connection off. A connection is only used when you open the shop or make a purchase, because that goes through Google Play Billing.

Can I lose a skin I paid for?

Not one you bought outright — those are one-time purchases tied to your Google account and restore when you reinstall. Only ships unlocked through a Pro subscription lock again if Pro lapses.

Is my high score sent anywhere?

Your best score is kept on the device, in the game's own save file. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Is it suitable for children?

It is rated Everyone: an abstract space shooter with no blood, no story and no chat. Worth knowing that it does carry in-app purchases, so a shared device is worth a Play Store purchase PIN.

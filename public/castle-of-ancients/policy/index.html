Privacy Policy — Castle of Ancients

Last updated: 2 September 2026

Castle of Ancients does not collect, transmit or share any information about you. It has no network access at all, no analytics, no advertising, no accounts and no in-app purchases. Everything it saves stays in the app's own folder on your device.

The short version

  • Nothing is collected. The game asks for no personal information and has no field to type any into.
  • Nothing is sent. The app declares no network capability, so there is no route by which anything could leave your device even by mistake.
  • No third parties. No advertising network, no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no social login.
  • No tracking. No advertising identifier is read, and nothing you do is linked to you or to any other app.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything. There is no server copy, because there was never a copy anywhere but your device.

What the app stores on your device

The game keeps one small settings file inside its own sandboxed folder. It is not readable by other apps, it is never uploaded, and it holds nothing that identifies you:

What Why it exists
Which missions you have cleared So the next story mission unlocks, and so Continue can put you back in the run you left.
Your settings Game speed, music and sound volume, language, control scheme, and the four campaign dials.
A local log file Written by the game engine to help diagnose a crash on your own device. It is never sent anywhere and you can delete it by deleting the app.

That is the whole list. There is no identifier, no timestamp tied to a person, and no usage history.

Permissions

The app requests no permissions. It does not use the camera, the microphone, the photo library, contacts, location, Bluetooth, local network or notifications, and it does not request tracking permission because it does not track.

If your device ever shows you a permission prompt attributed to this app, please treat it as a bug and tell us — it is not something the app is designed to do.

Children's privacy

The game is a cartoon-styled strategy game with no blood, no gore, no chat, no user-generated content and no link out of the app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone of any age, including children under 13, because we do not collect personal information at all.

There is nothing to buy inside the app, so there is no purchase a child could make by accident.

Your rights

Under GDPR, CCPA and equivalent laws you have rights of access, correction, deletion, portability and objection over your personal data. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing on our side for those requests to reach — but the rights still stand, and you are welcome to contact us to confirm exactly this.

  • Access: we hold no data about you to provide.
  • Deletion: removing the app deletes everything it saved.
  • Portability: there is no account and no server-side record to export.
  • Objection: there is no profiling, automated decision-making or targeted advertising in the app to object to.

Security

Because the app stores its data locally and transmits nothing, the usual risks to data in transit do not arise. The save file on your device is protected by your device's own security, so keep a screen lock on it. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, but there is no copy of anything anywhere else to be at risk.

Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time — for example if a later version of the app gains a feature that changes how data is handled. We will post the new policy on this page and update the "Last updated" date at the top.

You are encouraged to review this Policy periodically. Changes take effect when they are posted on this page.

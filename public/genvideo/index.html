The pipeline

Eight steps you do not have to do.

Every render walks the same road. You see it happen in the queue, and the app tells you what it is doing rather than showing a bar that means nothing.

Read the brief

A topic in a sentence, or your own script with one paragraph per scene. Both are first-class — a fixed script is used verbatim.

Write the narration

A language model turns the topic into scene-by-scene lines, inside the word range you set.

Draft image prompts

Each scene gets a prompt written for it, with your style prefix in front so the whole video looks like one piece.

Generate the artwork

One image per scene. Either a hosted model or your own ComfyUI — the choice is a setting, not a rebuild.

Speak the lines

Text to speech reads the narration in the voice you pick, including Vietnamese voices.

Lay out the frame

The template decides the layout and the output size: vertical for shorts, square, or landscape.

Add the music

A background track from the library or one you upload, mixed under the narration at the level you set.

Stitch the video

Scenes become segments, segments become one MP4, and it lands in the queue ready to play or save.

Questions

The things people actually ask.

Do I need an account?

No. A licence belongs to the machine, keyed by a random code the app generates on first run and keeps in the operating system's keychain. There is nothing to sign up for and no password to lose, and the server never learns your name or your email.

How long does one video take?

Roughly two to three minutes for a short two-scene video, and around five to ten for a longer one. Most of that is drawing the artwork — one image per scene — so a video with more scenes takes proportionally longer.

Can I use my own script instead of letting the AI write one?

Yes. Switch the mode to Fixed script and your text is used word for word, one scene per paragraph. Nothing is rewritten, and the word limits stop applying because they only govern what the model writes.

What happens when I cancel?

You keep the plan until the end of the period you already paid for. Cancelling does not cut you off mid-month — you paid for those days and they are yours. Cancel from PayPal or Google Play; the link is on the Plans screen.

Can I run it against my own image model?

Yes, on the Bring your own key plan. The app talks to a self-hosted ComfyUI or to your own OpenAI key, and picking which is a setting rather than a different build. It is also much cheaper per video, which is why that plan carries a far larger quota.

Does the video have a watermark?

Only on the free trial. Every paid plan renders clean.

What happens to the videos I make?

They are rendered on the server and stay there so the app can fetch them; download puts a copy on your device. The privacy page sets out exactly what is sent, what is kept and for how long.

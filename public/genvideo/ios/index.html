What it does

One idea in, one finished video out.

Eight steps run between your sentence and the file: the script, the prompts, the artwork, the voice, the layout, the music and the stitching. You watch them happen instead of doing them.

Write it, or let it write

Give it a topic and the narration is written for you, scene by scene. Or paste your own script and it is used word for word, one scene per paragraph.

31 templates

Vertical for shorts and reels, square for feeds, landscape for everything else. The template sets the layout and the output size in one choice.

Voices, including Vietnamese

The narration is read aloud in the voice you pick, and the whole interface speaks nine languages — not just the menus.

Watch it without leaving

Finished videos play inside the app with a scrub bar, and save straight to Photos when you want to keep one.

A queue you can trust

Every render with how long it took, how big it is and how long it runs. When one fails it says why, in plain words.

Your own music

Add a track and it sits under the narration at the level you choose, on this video and every one after it.

Support

A person answers.

Write in and you reach the developer, not a form. Include the licence code from the Plans screen — the app has no account and no email address on file, so that code is the only way to find your purchase.

Do I need an account?

No. Nothing to sign up for and no password to forget. A licence belongs to the device, identified by a random code the app makes on first run — it says nothing about you and is not built from your hardware.

How do I cancel?

Open Settings on your device, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and cancel there. It takes effect at the end of the period you have already paid for.

I changed phone. Do I lose my plan?

Use Restore purchases on the Plans screen and the App Store hands the subscription back to the app. Keeping a copy of your licence code is still worth doing.

Where do finished videos go?

They play in the app, and Download saves a copy to Photos. What is sent to make them, and how long it is kept, is set out on the privacy page.

How long does one video take?

Two to three minutes for a short one, five to ten for a longer one. Drawing the artwork is most of it — one image per scene — so more scenes means more time.

No ads. No trackers. Nothing collected.

It is the line every product here is measured against before it ships. Where something does need a server, the page for it says exactly what is sent, what is kept and for how long.

  • On the device

    Scanning, measurement and gameplay run locally. Nothing is uploaded to be analysed elsewhere.

  • No account

    Most products have nothing to sign up for, so there is no password to lose and no profile to leak.

  • Offline by default

    The games and the offline apps play through with the connection off.

  • Written down

    Every product carries its own privacy page, in plain language rather than a template.

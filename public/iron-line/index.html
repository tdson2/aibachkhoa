Towers

Four emplacements, four tiers each.

Every tower upgrades four times, retargets on demand, and sells back for credits when the shape of the battle changes. There is no single build that answers every map.

Ground and air

Fifteen ways in, and only one road.

Scouts, armoured hulls, shielded carriers and swarms follow the route you fortified. Drones, biplanes, jets and heavy bombers ignore it completely and fly straight at the core.

  • The road is only half the map. Five of the fifteen enemy types never touch it — they take the straight line to your core instead.
  • Rockets cannot elevate. An all-Rocket wall holds the road perfectly and loses the sky, so the line has to stay balanced.
  • They shoot back. Armoured hulls, serpents and bosses return fire on your emplacements, and bombers drop ordnance directly on top of them.
  • Towers have hull integrity. They patch themselves up between engagements and salvage part of their cost if they are destroyed — where you place one matters as much as which one you place.

Your pace

Call the wave, or take the minute.

Nothing runs on a timer you do not control. Call a wave early for bonus credits when the line is ready, or run the battle at 1x, 2x or 3x once it is set. Pause at any point.

  • Early-call bonus. Every wave you summon ahead of schedule pays extra credits into the same budget you are building with.
  • Three speeds. Once the line holds itself, run it at double or triple time rather than watching it work.
  • Ranked out of three. Keep the core untouched to earn all three marks. Progress saves on the device, with no account and no sync.

Privacy

It cannot phone home. There is no network code.

Iron Line is not built to collect anything, and it is not able to. The app ships without the internet permission, so there is no connection for data to leave through even if something tried.

Read the full privacy policy
  • No data collected. No account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising id, no crash reporting.
  • No third-party SDKs. Nothing from an ad network, an analytics vendor or a games service is linked into the build.
  • No internet permission. INTERNET is not declared in the manifest. The only permissions are VIBRATE and WAKE_LOCK.
  • Your save stays yours. Progress is written to the app's private storage on the device and is removed with the app.

Twelve battlefields. Nothing else asked of you.

Introduction

Dungeon of the Fallen Blade is a 2D pixel-art action roguelite for mobile: a hundred depths that change every run, a sword, a bow and a dodge roll, played on your own device, with optional leaderboards and achievements through Google Play Games Services.

Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

Types of Data Collected

Personal Data

The App does not ask You for any personally identifiable information. There is no account, no sign-up and no email address. The only data involved is:

  • Your Google Play Games profile — only if You choose to sign in to Play Games (see Sharing Data with Third Parties).
  • Game data kept on Your device: Your settings and Your best-depth record.

Usage Data

The App does not collect usage data for us. Specifically, it does not gather:

Device Information

  • Device identifiers such as IMEI, Android ID or Advertising ID.
  • Network status or connection details.

App Usage Data

  • How often or how long You play.
  • The actions You take within the App.
  • Crash logs or performance metrics.

Other Apps on Your Device

  • The App does not look at, list or inspect other apps installed on Your device.
  • No logs about Your device leave it.

Note: We do not collect personal files (e.g., photos, videos, contacts, messages) unless explicitly granted and clearly stated.

Google Play, from which You download the App, and Google Play Games Services, if You sign in, may process technical data such as Your IP address and device information under Google's own privacy policy. We do not receive that data.

We do not collect any information automatically when You use the App on a mobile device.

Visiting this website is separate from using the App; the App itself sends nothing to this website.

Sharing Data with Third Parties

The App integrates Google Play Games Services so that it can offer leaderboards and achievements. This is the only third-party service in the App. Signing in to Play Games is optional and is never required to play.

If You do sign in, the App sends Google three leaderboard scores — deepest descent, richest run and fastest descent — together with Your progress on eight achievements, tied to Your own Play Games profile. Google processes that data as an independent controller under its own privacy policy. If You do not sign in, nothing is sent.

We do not sell user data, and we do not share it with anyone beyond what is described above. There is no advertising, analytics or tracking in the App.

Permissions and Network Access

The App declares the Android INTERNET permission. It needs that permission for one purpose: reaching Google Play Games Services for the leaderboards and achievements described above.

The game itself does not require a connection. Every call to Play Games is skipped when You are not signed in, and a run plays identically with the connection off — You simply do not post a score while it is. The App requests no other sensitive permission: no location, no contacts, no storage access to Your personal files.

Data Storage and Deletion

We operate no servers of our own and do not collect or store user data on them. Everything the game needs in order to run — Your settings and Your best-depth record — is processed and stored locally on Your device. You can delete it by uninstalling the application or using the data deletion functions built into the application (if available).

The leaderboard scores and achievement progress described above are the exception: they are held by Google Play Games Services, not by us. You can review or remove them through Your Google Play Games account settings.

Sensitive Data

Dungeon of the Fallen Blade does not collect any sensitive user data, including but not limited to:

  • Location information
  • Contacts
  • Messages
  • Health data
  • Financial information

We are committed to not accessing, collecting or processing any sensitive user data.

User Control

You can uninstall the app at any time to stop all data access.

Use of Your Personal Data

Because the App collects no Personal Data for Us, the only data involved is used as follows:

  • To run the game: Your settings and best-depth record are used on Your device to play and to show Your progress.
  • For leaderboards and achievements: if You sign in to Play Games, Your scores and achievement progress are sent to Google so they appear on Your Play Games profile.
  • To reply to You: if You email Us, We use Your email address only to answer Your message.
  • We do not send marketing, news or special offers.
  • To manage Your requests: To attend and manage Your requests to Us.
  • For business transfers: We hold no user data from the App, so none would be transferred in a merger, sale or reorganisation.
  • We do not use Your information for analytics, usage trends, advertising or marketing.

We do not share Your personal information, except:

  • With Google Play Games Services: only if You sign in, as described under Sharing Data with Third Parties.
  • For business transfers: not applicable — We hold no user data from the App.
  • With Affiliates: none.
  • With business partners: none.
  • With other users: if You sign in to Play Games, Your leaderboard scores and Play Games name can be seen by other players on the leaderboards.
  • With Your consent: We may disclose Your personal information for any other purpose with Your consent.

In addition:

  • We use no service providers for analytics, crash reporting or advertising.
  • With law enforcement if required by applicable law.
  • If a business transfer ever happens, this policy continues to apply.
